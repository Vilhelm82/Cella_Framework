# Complementary paper — A use-case-agnostic role-cover calculus

**Status:** companion manuscript to `active_rechart_curvature_valuations.tex`.
**Rule of composition:** the main paper claims only what its gates prove; this companion
takes each result to its full proved scope and states the agnostic generalization,
keeping every unproved extension behind a named wall. Sources: the Part B sections of
the Stage 1–6 derivations (06_DERIVATIONS/), each backed by the same gates.

---

## 1. Object: constraint systems, not geometries

The entire calculus consumes one datum: a local law `F(x_0,...,x_n) = 0` among n+1
quantities over any field of characteristic 0, with a distinguished coordinate frame.
Nothing uses the ambient Euclidean structure until curvature is *interpreted*. The
dictionary:

| construct | agnostic meaning |
|---|---|
| output role | which quantity is currently solved for |
| active rechart | change of solved-for quantity (all sensitivities transform rationally) |
| passive relabelling | renaming inputs; provably contentless |
| regular locus | the regime where every quantity responds to every other |
| typed refusal | a quantity that locally cannot be an output |
| channel account | where the invariant sits in the declared frame |
| role-pair divisor | locus where a specific pair coupling of a specific chart degenerates |

## 2. The role groupoid at full scope (from Stage 1 B)

- The (n+1)-object groupoid of output charts, with rational triangular finite-jet
  transport localized at one denominator per morphism, is defined over any coefficient
  ring containing Q with the role denominators invertible: function fields, Puiseux
  series, parametric families.
- The ordered atlas is a principal S_{n+1}-space; active/passive separation is a theorem
  (passive slot actions fix chart germs; active transpositions act rationally and
  faithfully off the isotropy loci).
- Finite-tower naturality makes the calculus a functor on the projective system of jet
  orders — a projective-limit statement, never analytic convergence.
- Walls: characteristic p <= r needs divided powers; fully singular points carry stratum
  types, not jets; global monodromy of the cover is out of scope.

## 3. Channel accounts as torsors (from Stages 2, 4 B)

The account space over a fixed invariant value is an affine space modelled on the
zero-sum hyperplane. Both symmetry actions available to a constraint system act by
translations of this affine space:

- defining-function gauge (renormalizing the law): exact rational translation laws,
  tangent data invariant — Stage 2;
- active recharting (changing the output): affine kernel transport with exact cocycle —
  Stage 4.

Invariant content = the elementary symmetric flag sigma_1..sigma_{n-1}; everything else
is explanation. The graph theorem ("choosing an output kills interaction bookkeeping
locally") is a statement about presentations of laws, not about surfaces.

**Lorentzian coupling structure at all n (proved n=3; stated generally with the general
proof sketched):** pure-coupling data live on edge space R^(n(n-1)/2) — the same index
set as the gauge-normal quotient Sym_n/Im(G_g) ~= Q^(n(n-1)/2) — and the sigma_2-induced
quadratic form on it has exactly one negative direction (eigenvalues -(n-2) on the
all-ones vector, +2 on its complement). The null cone separates coherent from cancelling
coupling patterns. Gate-proved at n=3 (signature (2,1)); the n>3 spectral claim is
elementary linear algebra of 2I-J and is stated with proof in one line, but its
*channel-theoretic* faithfulness for r>=3 is a named wall.

## 4. The pair-form atlas (from Stage 3; the campaign's central new object)

For ANY constraint system, define chart-pair forms and pair forms

```
c_{i;{l,m}} = (q_i^2 / Lambda_{i,{l,m}}^2) (dx_i)^2 |_(T Sigma),
b_{lm}      = sum_{i not in {l,m}} c_{i;{l,m}}.
```

These are presentation-free. The entire role-indexed family of inverse-channel metrics
is generated by partial sums over pairs containing the graph role; defects are exact
pair-form coboundaries; the obstruction class vanishes; the half-sum descends. The
correct invariant object of "inverse-channel geometry" is therefore the **pair-form
atlas** `{b_{lm}}` — one canonical global form per unordered pair of roles — and every
presentation is a window onto it. The three-role case degenerates (weights become
presentation-free); the four-role witness proves the degeneration is an accident.

Agnostic reading: `b_{lm}` measures the inverse-square strength with which the pair
(l,m) is coupled through every third quantity. In a thermodynamic surface these are
pair-susceptibility forms; in an economic constraint they are pair-substitution forms.
The calculus is indifferent.

## 5. Valuations as conormal-weighted functors (from Stages 5, 6 B)

For the framed germ class near a boundary of the system:

- boundary blow-up rates of the curvature diagnostic are chart-free integers;
- leading amplitudes are sections of conormal powers (weight k), so amplitudes are
  comparable across models only in an intrinsic gauge (proper distance) or with the
  weight quoted;
- the determining data are finite weighted jets; transverse derivatives are excluded at
  leading order (proved with arbitrary function coefficients);
- degeneracies are equations, not exceptions: class boundary, unit failure, inverse
  minors, drift balance, frame activity.

Composition with §4: valuation data are functors of the germ; the germ is presentation-
indexed by the controlled pair-form rule; hence boundary diagnostics of any constraint
system are comparable across ALL of its presentations through the pair dictionary.
