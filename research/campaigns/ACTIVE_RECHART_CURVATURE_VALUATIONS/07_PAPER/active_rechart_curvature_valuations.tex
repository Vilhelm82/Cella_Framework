\begin{abstract}
\noindent For a regular constraint hypersurface $\Sig=\{F(x_0,\dots,x_n)=0\}$ we
assemble three previously separate calculi --- the active output-role jet calculus,
the framed curvature-channel account, and the local divisor valuation calculus ---
into one exact local theory. The central question, posed as a falsification gate
before any covariance theorem was built, is whether the chartwise inverse-channel
metrics $g_\rho$ are components of one tensor. The answer is negative in the
strongest controlled sense: strict descent fails on exact witnesses at three and
four roles, and the rechart defect $D_{\rho\sigma}=g_\rho-\varphi^*_{\rho\sigma}
g_\sigma$ is an \emph{exact coboundary of a canonical pair-indexed family of global
forms}, so the obstruction class of the role-metric atlas vanishes and ``the''
metric is replaced by a pair-form generated atlas. On top of this bridge we prove:
an $(n{+}1)$-role output-chart groupoid with exact rational finite-jet transport;
the affine kernel transport law for channel accounts; a gauge-normal-form quotient
$\mathrm{Sym}_n/\mathrm{Im}\,G_g\cong\mathbb{Q}^{n(n-1)/2}$ in every dimension; a
framed off-diagonal divisor germ class closed under admissible recharting with
rigid determinant valuation; and pole-order plus weight-$k$ principal-coefficient
naturality. The diagonal order-$3$/order-$4$ laws, the master quadric, the corner
calculus, the three output-role quadruples, and the Kerr--Newman $3/4/4$ pattern
are recovered as strict corollaries. Every claim is backed by an exact symbolic
gate; every gate is reproducible from the release package.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The predecessor papers left a specific bridge open. Paper~I established the active
$S_3$ role-jet calculus and the three-channel account of Gaussian curvature on a
three-role relation; Paper~II established the local valuation calculus of diagonal
divisor-monomial metrics; the Kerr--Newman application selected an inverse-channel
metric whose scalar-curvature valuations retrodict the physical role divisors. None
of them answered: \emph{what happens to the metric assignment itself under active
output-role recharting?} A metric diagonal in one native role chart is off-diagonal
in every other; if the chartwise metrics were silently assumed to be one tensor,
the generalized theory would rest on an unproved covariance. This paper makes the
descent question the first theorem-grade gate and builds only on its proved answer.

Conventions are frozen once: the invariant is $\sigma_2(S)=\tfrac12((\operatorname{tr}
S)^2-\operatorname{tr}S^2)$ of the tangent shape operator, with $\sigma_2=K_G$ for
surfaces and $R=2\sigma_2$ for Euclidean hypersurfaces; the canonical channel order
is $(\sigma_2;\kc,\ki,\ks)$, and legacy tuples $(K_G,\kc,\ks,\ki)$ convert by
swapping the last two entries. The round unit two-sphere has $R=+2$.

\section{Regular output-role covers}
Let $F\in C^{r+1}$, $\Sig=\{F=0\}$, and
$\Om=\{F=0,\ \prod_i F_i\neq0\}$, $F_i=\partial F/\partial x_i$.

\begin{theorem}[role cover and finite-jet groupoid]\label{thm:A}
On $\Om$ every coordinate is locally an output: the $n{+}1$ output charts
$x_\rho=f_\rho(x_{j\neq\rho})$ cover a neighbourhood of any point in $\Sig$. For
each pair $\rho\neq\sigma$ the induced jet transport
$T_{\rho\sigma}:J^r_\rho\to J^r_\sigma$ is rational in the input jet, triangular in
the degree filtration (degree-$d$ output coefficients depend on input coefficients
of degree $\le d$ only), with all denominators powers of the single first-order
role denominator $a=\partial f_\rho/\partial x_\sigma$ ($a^{2d-1}$ at degree $d$);
identity, inverse and triple-overlap composition laws hold exactly, and truncation
commutes with the action at every finite order. For a fully labelled role set the
ordered charts form a principal $S_{n+1}$-space generated by passive slot swaps
(which fix every chart germ) and active output transpositions (which change the jet
rationally); the quotient by the passive $S_n$ is the $(n{+}1)$-object output-role
cover, and at $n{+}1=3$ the action is the exact birational $S_3$ of Paper~I.
\end{theorem}

Where a role denominator vanishes the corresponding morphism is deleted and the
point is a typed stratum; the transport engine refuses rather than coerces.
\emph{Gates:} \texttt{role\_cover\_groupoid/} ($r=2$: seven checks; $r=3$: five
checks including the CCE-8 finite-tower replay; Gate~1 recovery of all ten legacy
inverse-chart derivatives).

\section{Curvature normalization and channel reduction}
With $g=\nabla F$, $H=\nabla^2F$, $q=g^Tg$, and the frame split $H=H_c+H_s$
($H_s=\operatorname{diag}H$), the bordered channel density
\[
\widehat{\mathcal C}_2(t,u)=-\!\!\sum_{|I|=3}\det\begin{pmatrix}0&g_I^T\\
g_I&(tH_c+uH_s)_I\end{pmatrix}
\]
defines normalized channels $\kappa=\widehat\kappa/q^2$ with account
$C=(\sigma_2;\kc,\ki,\ks)$ and exact sequence
$0\to\ker\Sigma\to\text{Account}\to\sigma_2\to0$. The account is a lift of the
invariant through the affine fibre $\Sigma^{-1}(\sigma_2)$ --- framed data, not a
new scalar invariant.

\begin{theorem}[normalization]\label{thm:B}
The channel sum equals $\sigma_2$ of the true tangent shape operator in every
ambient dimension (verified independently at $n=3,4$), and $R=2\sigma_2$ for
Euclidean graph hypersurfaces (verified intrinsically in dimensions $2,3$). In any
graph chart of a surface, $\kc=-M^2/Q^2$, $\ks=LN/Q^2$, $\ki=0$; interaction is
presentation data, live exactly off graph gauges (keystone witness:
$(\kc,\ki,\ks)=(-\tfrac1{49},-\tfrac3{49},\tfrac1{49})$).
\end{theorem}

The defining-function gauge $\widetilde F=\mu F$ ($\mu(p)=1$,
$a=\nabla\log\mu$) shifts $\widetilde H=H+ga^T+ag^T$; the tangent projector kills
the shift, so every $\sigma_r$ is gauge-invariant while the account moves strictly
inside $\ker\Sigma$ by exact rational laws. \emph{Gates:}
\texttt{channel\_normalization/} (fourteen checks; a harness/paper conflict at the
keystone gauge law was resolved \emph{against the harness} --- symbol collision ---
and is kept on record).

\section{Gauge normal form in all dimensions}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:gnf}
For $g_1\cdots g_n\neq0$, $a_i=H_{ii}/(2g_i)$ is the unique gauge with
$\operatorname{diag}(H-G_g(a))=0$, $G_g(a)=ga^T+ag^T$; the representative's
off-diagonal entries are the gauge-invariant obstruction coordinates
$O_{ij}=H_{ij}-g_iH_{jj}/(2g_j)-g_jH_{ii}/(2g_i)$, and
$\mathrm{Sym}_n/\mathrm{Im}\,G_g\cong\mathbb{Q}^{n(n-1)/2}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{lemma}[Lorentzian coupling edge]
With $\nu=(g_1H_{23},g_2H_{13},g_3H_{12})$ and $J=\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^T$:
$\kc=-\nu^T(2I-J)\nu/q^2$, $\operatorname{sig}(2I-J)=(2,1)$; for a single-axis
gauge $a=te_i$ and \emph{arbitrary} $H$,
$\delta\kc=4t\,g_1g_2g_3H_{jk}/q^2$, so $te_i$ pins $\kc$ iff $H_{jk}=0$.
\end{lemma}

\section{Active channel transport}
\begin{theorem}[affine kernel transport]\label{thm:C}
Under active recharting the account of the target chart is obtained by
transporting the jet and re-evaluating the graph-channel formula, and
\[
C_\sigma=C_\rho+Z_{\rho\sigma},\qquad Z_{\rho\sigma}\in\ker\Sigma,\qquad
Z_{\rho\tau}=Z_{\rho\sigma}+Z_{\sigma\tau},
\]
identically in the generic symbolic jet, with $Z\neq0$ generically: of the three
candidate structures (strict/affine/obstructed), the proved outcome is
\emph{affine} with identity action on the invariant part.
\end{theorem}

The three output-role quadruples, both closure identities, the transverse quadratic
$R_{\mathrm{trans}}=a^2C-2abB+b^2A$ with $\kappa_{s,D}=AR_{\mathrm{trans}}/(a^2q_0^2)$,
$\kappa_{s,S}=CR_{\mathrm{trans}}/(b^2q_0^2)$, the worked example
$(a,b,A,B,C)=(5,7,2,3,4)$, and the carrier faithfulness Jacobian
$8\Lambda_P\Lambda_D\Lambda_S/q_0^6$ are recovered exactly from this machinery
(Gate~4). Input swaps fix the scalar accounts while moving the labelled jet: the
sixfold object collapses to three accounts, as it must.

\section{Metric descent and its replacement}\label{sec:descent}
For presentation $\rho$ (role $x_\rho$ as graph value) the generalized
inverse-channel assignment is
\[
g_\rho=\sum_{i\neq\rho}G^{(\rho)}_i\,(dx_i)^2,\qquad
G^{(\rho)}_i=q_i^2\!\!\sum_{m\notin\{i,\rho\}}\!\Lambda_{i,\{\rho,m\}}^{-2},
\]
with $q_i$ the $i$-chart norm and $\Lambda_{i,\{l,m\}}=\partial^2f_i/\partial
x_l\partial x_m$ the \emph{chart-intrinsic} pair couplings.

\begin{theorem}[descent falsified; pair-form generation]\label{thm:E}
Strict descent fails: $D_{\rho\sigma}=g_\rho-\varphi^*_{\rho\sigma}g_\sigma\neq0$
on exact witnesses at three and four roles, and the pullback is not conformal to
the native metric. Define the presentation-free forms
\[
c_{i;\{l,m\}}=\frac{q_i^2}{\Lambda_{i,\{l,m\}}^2}(dx_i)^2\Big|_{T\Sig},\qquad
b_{lm}=\sum_{i\notin\{l,m\}}c_{i;\{l,m\}} .
\]
Then, exactly and at every role count tested (three and four roles, generic
symbolic and exact rational):
\[
g_\rho=\sum_{m\neq\rho}b_{\rho m},\qquad
D_{\rho\sigma}=\!\!\sum_{m\notin\{\rho,\sigma\}}\!\!(b_{\rho m}-b_{\sigma m}),\qquad
T:=\tfrac12\sum_\rho g_\rho=\sum_{\{l,m\}}b_{lm}.
\]
The defect is an exact coboundary; the \v Cech obstruction class of the atlas in
$H^1(\text{role cover},\mathrm{Sym}^2T^*\Sig)$ vanishes; the cocycle law holds in
plain and twisted form. Descending data: signature, the pair forms, the half-sum
$T$, the determinant divisor class (up to chart-Jacobian squares), the role-pair
divisors. Non-descending: the metric, its conformal class, the determinant
function.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
At $n=2$ the weights $q_i^2/\Lambda_i^2$ are presentation-independent and
$g_\rho=T-c_\rho$ with rank-one $c_\rho$; this is a three-role accident. At four
roles the shared-coordinate entries genuinely differ across presentations (exact
witness), and only the pair-resolved objects are canonical. ``The DBP metric''
(definite article) is hereby retired in favour of the pair-form atlas.
\end{remark}

(twenty-four checks). \emph{Report:} \texttt{REPORT\_01\_METRIC\_DESCENT\_OR\_OBSTRUCTION.md}.

\section{Framed off-diagonal divisor germs}
The valuation-stable class near a smooth divisor $D=\{z=0\}$ is
$g=E^T\operatorname{diag}(z^{p_i}h_i)E$ with $E$ smooth invertible and $h_i$ smooth
nonvanishing up to $D$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:class}
(i) The class is closed under boundary-adapted recharts with unchanged exponent
vector. (ii) $\det g=z^{\sum p_i}(\det E)^2\prod h_i$: the determinant valuation is
rigid inside the class. (iii) $\operatorname{ord}_z(g^{-1})_{ij}\ge-\max_kp_k$ with
equality on the stratum $E^{-1}_{ik^*}E^{-1}_{jk^*}|_D\neq0$; off it the valuation
jumps (exact witness: $-4\to0$ with $\det$ rigid). (iv)
$\operatorname{ord}_zR\ge2(\min p-\max p)-2$, and the leading term is determined by
a finite jet of $(E,h)$ (perturbations at $z^5$ change nothing). (v) At $E=I$ the
diagonal calculus is recovered verbatim.
\end{theorem}

Degeneracy strata are named algebraically (class boundary $\det E|_D=0$; unit
failure; inverse-cancellation minors; drift balance $\sum P_{\alpha1}/P_{\alpha0}=0$
dropping order $3\to2$; curvature-active frame dressing) --- typed strata, never
exception prose. Off-diagonal members do \emph{not} inherit diagonal Newton
vertices (exact instances); leading-term claims must pass through the weighted
initial form. \emph{Gates:} \texttt{off\_diagonal\_germs/} (four gates).

\section{Curvature valuation naturality}
\begin{theorem}[pole order and principal coefficient]\label{thm:D}
Along a smooth collapsing divisor of a class member: (1) the scalar-curvature pole
order is invariant under admissible recharting; (2) if $z'=u(y)z+O(z^2)$,
$u|_D\neq0$, and $R=c(y)z^{-k}+O(z^{-k+1})$, then $c'=u^kc$, and only $u|_D$
enters: the principal coefficient is a section of the $k$-th conormal power, not a
scalar; (3) the principal term depends on a finite weighted jet only; (4) at
leading order no transverse derivative of any coefficient function appears
(generic face $(P_1/P_0+R_1/R_0)/A_2\,z^{-3}$; reflection face $-m(m+5)/B\,z^{-4}$,
proved with arbitrary symbolic function coefficients on an engine independent of
the legacy verifier); (5) cancellation branches are the typed strata of
Theorem~\ref{thm:class}.
\end{theorem}

Intrinsic packaging: $\lim R\,d^2=-m(m+5)/4$ (parity) and
$\lim R\,d^{3/2}=\Theta A^{-1/4}/2^{3/2}$ (generic) in proper distance $d$; a
quadratic rechart term generates the odd layer $4m(m{+}5)k_2/(Bc^5)$ --- parity
preservation is an odd-coordinate condition. \emph{Gates:}
\texttt{valuation\_naturality/} (four gates, including the packaged legacy
weighted-jet proof replayed with its shim path fixed).

\section{The three-role surface theorem}
The quadruples
\[
C_P=\Bigl(K,-\tfrac{B^2}{q_0^2},\tfrac{AC}{q_0^2},0\Bigr),\quad
C_D=\Bigl(K,-\tfrac{(Ab-aB)^2}{a^2q_0^2},\tfrac{A(Ab^2-2abB+a^2C)}{a^2q_0^2},0\Bigr),\quad
C_S=\Bigl(K,-\tfrac{(Ca-bB)^2}{b^2q_0^2},\tfrac{C(Ca^2-2abB+b^2A)}{b^2q_0^2},0\Bigr)

labelled sixfold carrier survives at jet level only.

\section{Kerr--Newman replay}
With $M^2=U(S,J,Q)$ on the outer wedge, the campaign machinery reproduces the
selected metric's entries exactly from chart-pair data; the extremal boundary is a
generic quadratic collapse (with the paper's $A_2$, whose $U_S^2$ term drops at

strict descent fails at exact points, pair-form generation holds. The strict
negativity of the extremal coefficient and the exact reflection coefficients
