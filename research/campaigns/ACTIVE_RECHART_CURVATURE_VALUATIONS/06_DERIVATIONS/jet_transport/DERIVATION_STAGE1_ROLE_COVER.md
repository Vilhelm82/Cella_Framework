## Part A — scoped to what the paper requires

### A.1 Objects

Let `F in C^{r+1}(U)`, `U ⊂ R^{n+1}` open with coordinates `(x_0,...,x_n)`, and

```
Sigma      = {F = 0},
Omega_role = {F = 0,  prod_{i=0}^{n} F_i != 0},   F_i = dF/dx_i.
```

**Definition A.1 (output chart).** For a role `rho`, the `rho`-output chart at
`p in Omega_role` is the unique local solution `x_rho = f_rho(x_{j != rho})` given by the
implicit function theorem (`F_rho(p) != 0`). Its **based r-jet** `J^r_rho` is the degree-r
Taylor polynomial of `f_rho` at `p` with the base point translated to the origin.

**Definition A.2 (transition).** For `rho != sigma`, the chart transition on the base is

```
phi_(rho,sigma) : (x_j)_{j != rho}  |->  (x_j)_{j != sigma},
     x_rho-slot filled by f_rho,  all shared coordinates passed through.
```

Note `phi_(rho,sigma)` needs only `f_rho`; it is the identity map of `Sigma` read between two
graph presentations.

### A.2 Theorem A (regular output-role cover and finite-jet action)

**(i) Existence (PO-1).** On `Omega_role` every coordinate serves locally as an output; the
`n+1` output charts form an open cover of a neighbourhood of `p` in `Sigma`.

**(ii) Rational finite-jet transport (PO-2).** For each pair `rho != sigma` there is a map
`T_(rho,sigma) : J^r_rho -> J^r_sigma` computed by formal inversion, with:

- first order:  `d f_sigma / d x_rho = 1/a`,  `d f_sigma / d x_j = -c_j / a`  where
  `a = d f_rho / d x_sigma`, `c_j = d f_rho / d x_j` (j != rho, sigma);
- second order (proved and gate-checked in the three-role case; general case via the same
  substitution): every degree-2 coefficient is rational with denominator `a^3`;
- degree d: the coefficient is rational in the input jet through degree d only, with
  denominator `a^(2d-1)` (gate-checked at d = 2, 3; general d by the homogeneous inverse
  recurrence of Paper I Appendix A, whose induction is role-count-independent).

**(iii) Groupoid laws (PO-3).** On the common regular locus,

```
T_(rho,rho) = id,     T_(sigma,rho) ∘ T_(rho,sigma) = id,
T_(sigma,tau) ∘ T_(rho,sigma) = T_(rho,tau).
```

Proof: each `T` is jet-truncation of composition with the identity map of `Sigma`
re-presented; composition of the underlying germs is associative and the based truncation
commutes with it at every finite order (CCE-8 naturality, replayed at H4). The gates verify
the laws directly on generic symbolic 3-jets (three roles) and random exact-Q 3-jets
(four roles).

**(iv) Active/passive separation (PO-4).** The ordered-chart object at order r is the pair
(output role, ordered input slots). Passive relabelling permutes slots and renames indices:
it fixes every chart germ (r2 gate G6, recovery gate "input swap fixes the P-output germ").
Active transposition re-solves the relation and changes the jet rationally (`A -> -A/a^3`,
witnessed exactly). For `n+1` fully labelled roles the ordered charts form a torsor over
`S_{n+1}` generated by slot swaps (passive) and output transpositions (active); the quotient
by the passive subgroup `S_n` of slot permutations is the `(n+1)`-object output-role cover.
At `n+1 = 3` this is exactly the birational `S_3` of Paper I: gates G4/H1 verify
`s^2 = t^2 = e`, `(st)^3 = e` on generic symbolic jets at orders 2 and 3.

**(v) Denominator locus.** All denominators of `T_(rho,sigma)` at order r are powers of the
single first-order role denominator `a = d f_rho / d x_sigma = -F_sigma / F_rho`; hence on
`Omega_role` the entire groupoid is defined, and exact-Q jets stay exact-Q localized at
`prod F_i` (rational closure). Outside `Omega_role` the rechart is a **typed refusal**
(gate G7), not a coerced value.

## Part B — complementary expansion (full coverage; use-case-agnostic)

### B.1 The groupoid, intrinsically

Let `k` be any field of characteristic 0 (the gates run over `Q`; the inversion recurrence
divides only by `a` and by factorials through r — see B.4 for positive characteristic).
Nothing in A.2 uses the ambient Euclidean structure: the input is a formal/`C^{r+1}` germ
of a regular hypersurface with a **distinguished coordinate frame**. The correct general
carrier is:

- objects: roles `0..n` (equivalently, the `n+1` graph presentations of one germ);
- morphisms `rho -> sigma`: the jet transports `T_(rho,sigma)`;
- structure group at an object: passive slot permutations `S_n` (jet-fixing);
- the full ordered atlas is a principal `S_{n+1}`-space: `(n+1)!` ordered charts,
  `n+1` germs.

**Theorem B.1 (localized rational representation).** `T_(rho,sigma)` at order r is a
morphism of affine spaces over the localization `k[J^r_rho][a^{-1}]`, polynomial of degree
`<= 2r-1` in `a^{-1}`, triangular in the degree filtration (degree-d output coefficients
are functions of input coefficients of degree `<= d` only — gate H5). Consequently the
groupoid action extends verbatim to:

- any coefficient ring `R ⊇ Q` with `a` invertible (jets over function fields, Puiseux
  series, parametric families);
- the projective limit `r -> infinity` of formal germs (finite-tower naturality, CCE-8;
  this is a projective-system statement, **not** analytic convergence).

### B.2 What is genuinely new beyond the three-role case

1. **Composition replaces word reduction.** For `n+1 = 3`, all transports are words in
   `{s,t}`; for general `n+1` the groupoid has `n(n+1)` elementary transpositions whose
   consistency is exactly the triple-overlap law A.2(iii) — verified independently of any
   presentation by generators.
2. **Denominator bookkeeping is per-pair.** The transport `rho -> sigma` is localized at
   `F_sigma/F_rho` only. A path `rho -> sigma -> tau` is a priori localized at two
   denominators, but the composite equals the direct transport localized at one: the extra
   locus is removable. (This is the jet-level seed of the Stage 6 statement that valuative
   data glue over the cover even when intermediate charts degenerate.)
3. **Typed singular strata.** `F_sigma = 0` with `F_rho != 0` deletes the morphism
   `rho -> sigma` but not the objects; the nerve of the cover changes type along the
   stratification by which `F_i` vanish. The role cover is a constructible groupoid, not a
   fixed group.

### B.3 Use-case dictionary (agnostic form)

The construction consumes any local law relating `n+1` quantities:

| ingredient | agnostic meaning |
|---|---|
| `F = 0` | any constraint among observables/state variables/couplings |
| output role | which quantity is currently solved for |
| active rechart | change of solved-for quantity (changes all sensitivities) |
| passive relabelling | renaming inputs (changes nothing) |
| `Omega_role` | the regime where every quantity responds to every other |
| refusal stratum | a quantity that locally cannot be an output (conservation, saturation) |

Thermodynamic fundamental relations (Stage 8) are one instance; production functions,
reaction networks, and any smooth constitutive law are others, with no change to a single
formula above.

### B.4 Named boundaries (walls, with causes)

- **Positive characteristic:** the degree-d inverse coefficient divides by d! through the
  Taylor normalization; for char p <= r the based-jet coordinatization itself needs divided
  powers. The `S_3` relations survive as maps of divided-power jets; this campaign does not
  use them (Campaign H char-2 material stays outside — governance rule).
- **Non-reduced / singular `Sigma`:** no output chart exists at points where all `F_i = 0`;
  the object there is the stratum type, not a jet.
- **Global gluing:** the groupoid is local; monodromy of the role cover around the singular
  strata is explicitly out of scope for the first paper (plan §4).
