# Development Plan: Active Recharting and Curvature Valuations of Framed Constraint Hypersurfaces

## Working subtitle

**Role Covers, Tensorial Channel Transport, and Divisor Normal Forms**

**Campaign status:** proposed successor programme  
**Date opened:** 2026-07-30  
**Primary target:** a generalized local theorem joining the active role-jet calculus to the local curvature-valuation calculus  
**Recommended manuscript form:** a new additive manuscript until the bridge theorem is proved; only then decide whether it replaces Paper I, replaces the general half of Paper II, or becomes a new foundational paper above both.

## 1. Executive decision

The next body of work should not be another Kerr-Newman-specific calculation and should not be folded into the DBP curvature-period paper.

The correct target is the currently missing bridge:

```text
active output-role recharting
        +
framed curvature-channel accounting
        +
local divisor valuation calculus
        =
a generalized role-cover curvature calculus
```

The central open problem is to determine how a curvature-bearing metric and its local divisor asymptotics behave under **active output-role recharting**, where a metric that is diagonal in one native role chart will generally become off-diagonal in another.

The campaign must not assume in advance that the chartwise inverse-channel metrics are components of one global tensor. The first decisive gate is to calculate the rechart defect and classify the outcome:

```text
Defect_(rho,sigma)
    = g_rho - phi_(rho,sigma)^*(g_sigma).
```

There are three acceptable theorem-grade outcomes:

1. **Strict descent:** the defect vanishes and the chartwise metrics are one tensor.
2. **Controlled transport:** the defect is nonzero but belongs to an explicit gauge, conformal, or channel-kernel class satisfying a cocycle law.
3. **Intrinsic non-descent:** no single tensor exists, but the role-indexed metric atlas and its obstruction class are canonical.

A negative descent result is not a failure. It would identify the exact geometric object that replaces the hoped-for tensor and prevent the generalized theory from being built on a false covariance assumption.

## 2. Intended canonical outcome

### Working theorem family

The completed paper should establish a theorem family of the following form.

#### Theorem A: regular output-role cover and finite-jet action

Let

```text
Sigma = {F(x_0,...,x_n) = 0}
```

be a regular constraint hypersurface, and restrict to the locus

```text
Omega_role
    = {F = 0 and product_i F_i != 0}.
```

Every coordinate can then be used locally as an output. For each output role `rho`, solve

```text
x_rho = f_rho(x_0,...,hat{x_rho},...,x_n).
```

The transition between any two output charts induces a rational finite-jet map

```text
T_(rho,sigma) : J^r_rho -> J^r_sigma
```

on the regular locus, with exact identity and composition laws.

This should generalize the existing three-role birational `S_3` action to the appropriate output-chart groupoid for `n+1` roles. For a fully labelled role set it reduces to the permutation action generated by active inverse-function recharting, not passive index relabelling.

#### Theorem B: framed curvature-channel account

Let `S` be the shape operator in a chosen framed chart and split it into the coupling and self parts

```text
S = S_c + S_s.
```

Use the second elementary curvature

```text
sigma_2(S)
    = 1/2 * ((tr S)^2 - tr(S^2)).
```

This normalization is essential:

```text
surface dimension 2:
    sigma_2(S) = K_G

Euclidean hypersurface of any dimension:
    scalar curvature R = 2 * sigma_2(S).
```

Define

```text
kappa_c
    = 1/2 * ((tr S_c)^2 - tr(S_c^2))

kappa_s
    = 1/2 * ((tr S_s)^2 - tr(S_s^2))

kappa_int
    = (tr S_c)(tr S_s) - tr(S_c S_s).
```

Then

```text
sigma_2(S) = kappa_c + kappa_int + kappa_s.
```

The channel vector is

```text
Kappa = (kappa_c, kappa_int, kappa_s).
```

The reduction map is

```text
Sigma_channel(Kappa)
    = kappa_c + kappa_int + kappa_s.
```

A chart account is therefore a lift of the invariant curvature through the affine fibre

```text
Sigma_channel^(-1)(sigma_2).
```

This makes the channel decomposition a framed account, not an additional scalar invariant.

#### Theorem C: active rechart transport or obstruction

For each output chart `rho`, define its channel account

```text
C_rho
    = (sigma_2; kappa_c,rho, kappa_int,rho, kappa_s,rho).
```

The existing papers often use the legacy order

```text
(K_G, kappa_c, kappa_s, kappa_int).
```

The new manuscript should freeze the canonical order as

```text
(sigma_2; kappa_c, kappa_int, kappa_s)
```

and include a one-line conversion table.

Under active recharting, prove one of the following precise structures:

```text
strict:
    C_sigma = T_(rho,sigma) * C_rho

affine:
    C_sigma = T_(rho,sigma) * C_rho + Z_(rho,sigma),
    with Z_(rho,sigma) in ker(Sigma_channel)

obstructed:
    {C_rho} is a canonical role-indexed family whose transition defects
    form a nontrivial cocycle in the channel-kernel bundle.
```

In every case the total curvature must satisfy

```text
Sigma_channel(C_sigma)
    = Sigma_channel(C_rho)
```

after the ordinary scalar pullback is accounted for.

The transition defect should satisfy

```text
Z_(rho,tau)
    = Z_(rho,sigma) + T_(rho,sigma) Z_(sigma,tau)
```

whenever all three charts overlap.

#### Theorem D: off-diagonal divisor valuation naturality

Let `D = {z = 0}` be a smooth collapsing divisor and let the metric be nondegenerate with fixed signature on one punctured side.

The initial off-diagonal class should be a framed monomial-unit germ:

```text
g(z,y)
    = E(z,y)^T
      diag(z^p_1 h_1(z,y), ..., z^p_m h_m(z,y))
      E(z,y),
```

where:

```text
E extends smoothly and invertibly to D,
h_i extends smoothly and is nonzero on D.
```

This class is broad enough to contain smooth off-diagonal rechart pullbacks of the existing diagonal normal forms, while retaining a controllable valuation structure.

The theorem should establish:

```text
1. pole order of scalar curvature is invariant under admissible recharting;

2. the leading coefficient has an explicit defining-function scaling law;

3. the principal curvature term depends only on a finite weighted jet;

4. the diagonal divisor-monomial theorem is the E = I specialization;

5. cancellation and degeneracy strata are named algebraically rather than
   hidden under a genericity phrase.
```

If

```text
z' = u(y) z + O(z^2),
u|_D != 0,
```

and

```text
R[g] = c(y) z^(-k) + O(z^(-k+1)),
```

then the new proof must derive the exact transformation of `c`, rather than treating it as an ordinary scalar. The expected form is a weight-`k` normal coefficient:

```text
c'(y) = u(y)^k c(y)
```

subject to the convention used for the chart transition. This should be packaged intrinsically as a principal coefficient attached to a power of the normal line, so the statement does not depend on a silently fixed boundary coordinate.

#### Theorem E: active rechart-valuation bridge

Let a role-jet metric construction be denoted by

```text
M : J^r_role -> Met.
```

For each output role,

```text
g_rho = M(J_rho).
```

The bridge theorem must determine whether

```text
g_rho = phi_(rho,sigma)^*(g_sigma)
```

holds, and if it does not, compute

```text
D_(rho,sigma)
    = g_rho - phi_(rho,sigma)^*(g_sigma).
```

The final theorem should state exactly which of the following quantities are natural across the role cover:

```text
metric tensor,
conformal class,
signature,
determinant divisor,
scalar-curvature pole order,
principal curvature coefficient,
channel account,
channel-kernel defect,
weighted initial form.
```

No item should be declared natural merely because total Gaussian curvature was invariant in the three-role surface calculation.

## 3. Existing results that must be recovered as corollaries

The generalized paper is successful only if it mechanically reproduces the existing closed results without re-deriving each application from scratch.

### 3.1 Three output-role chart quadruples

For

```text
P = f(D,S)

a = f_D
b = f_S
A = f_DD
B = f_DS
C = f_SS

q0 = 1 + a^2 + b^2
K  = (A*C - B^2) / q0^2,
```

the legacy chart quadruples are

```text
C_P =
(
    K,
    -B^2 / q0^2,
    A*C / q0^2,
    0
)

C_D =
(
    K,
    -(A*b - a*B)^2 / (a^2*q0^2),
    A*(A*b^2 - 2*a*b*B + a^2*C) / (a^2*q0^2),
    0
)

C_S =
(
    K,
    -(C*a - b*B)^2 / (b^2*q0^2),
    C*(C*a^2 - 2*a*b*B + b^2*A) / (b^2*q0^2),
    0
).
```

The generalized proof must recover the two closure identities

```text
A*(A*b^2 - 2*a*b*B + a^2*C) - (A*b - a*B)^2
    = a^2*(A*C - B^2)

C*(C*a^2 - 2*a*b*B + b^2*A) - (C*a - b*B)^2
    = b^2*(A*C - B^2).
```

It must also recover the structural collapse

```text
six ordered role charts
    -> three scalar output-role accounts
```

while retaining the labelled sixfold object at the jet level.

### 3.2 Gauge and reduction results

The successor must preserve or reprove:

```text
0 -> ker(Sigma_channel) -> ChannelAccount -> IntrinsicCurvature -> 0
```

together with:

```text
defining-function gauge:
    F_tilde = mu F

point-normalized Hessian shift:
    H_tilde = H + g a^T + a g^T

tangent projection:
    P(g a^T + a g^T)P = 0.
```

It must also absorb the unique gauge-normal-form quotient proof

```text
Sym_3 / Im(G_g) ~= Q^3
```

in the three-role case, or state and prove its correct all-dimensional replacement.

### 3.3 Diagonal local curvature laws

The new theorem must recover the current diagonal theory:

```text
generic collapse:
    g = diag(
        A2*x^2 + A3*x^3 + O(x^4),
        P0 + P1*x + O(x^2),
        R0 + R1*x + O(x^2)
    )

    R[g]
      = ((P1/P0) + (R1/R0))/A2 * x^(-3)
        + O(x^(-2)).
```

On the nondegenerate stratum:

```text
P1/P0 + R1/R0 != 0.
```

For the reflection-fixed germ:

```text
g = diag(
        B*x^2 + B4*x^4 + O(x^6),
        C1*x^(-2) + C10 + O(x^2),
        C2*x^(-2) + C20 + O(x^2)
    )

R[g] = -14/B * x^(-4) + O(x^(-3)).
```

In fibre dimension `m`, recover

```text
R[g] = -m*(m+5)/B * x^(-4) + lower order.
```

The proof must preserve the variable-transverse strengthening:

```text
leading coefficient contains no transverse derivative
```

under the stated weighted-valuation hypotheses.

### 3.4 Newton and corner laws

The existing diagonal codimension-two Newton polygon, codimension-`k` base-support rule, and cancellation hierarchy should remain available as corollaries or a retained appendix.

The new off-diagonal theorem should initially claim only what is proved. Recommended scope:

```text
new off-diagonal theorem:
    one smooth divisor, arbitrary state-space dimension

retained diagonal theorem:
    normal crossings of arbitrary codimension

optional extension:
    off-diagonal codimension two after the one-divisor theorem closes
```

Do not advertise a general off-diagonal codimension-`k` theorem unless its determinant, inverse-metric, and cancellation structure are actually controlled.

### 3.5 Kerr-Newman realization

The general theorem should recover, as application-level corollaries:

```text
T = 0        -> generic order 3
Omega = 0    -> reflection-fixed order 4
Phi_e = 0    -> reflection-fixed order 4
Omega=Phi_e=0 -> mixed corner, minimum order 2 on balanced approach
```

The strict-negative extremal coefficient and the exact reflection coefficients remain in the Kerr-Newman application paper. The generalized paper should explain why their orders and structural forms follow from the local theorem, not duplicate every physical factorization.

### Curved ambient extension

For a curved ambient space, use the Gauss equation:

```text
R_Sigma
    = R_ambient,tangent
      + (tr S)^2 - tr(S^2).
```

The extrinsic term channelizes because it is quadratic in `S`. The ambient tangential curvature term must remain a separate input unless the ambient geometry supplies its own compatible role frame.

A constant-curvature or symmetric-space extension may be added only after the flat-ambient theorem is closed.

## Stage 3: determine whether the inverse-channel metric descends

### Objective

Resolve the key ambiguity before building a covariance theorem on top of it.

### Tasks

For every role overlap, compute:

```text
D_(rho,sigma)
    = g_rho - phi_(rho,sigma)^*(g_sigma).
```

Classify the defect:

```text
D = 0
D = lambda*g
D = Lie/gauge-exact term
D lies in a channel-kernel subspace
D is nonzero with no controlled reduction
```

Test the generic symbolic three-role jet first. Do not start with Kerr-Newman.

### Decision tree

#### Branch A: strict descent

- Is the ambient metric flat, fixed-signature, or merely nondegenerate?
- Does the divisor map to the same geometric divisor under recharting?

- Are all rechart denominators named?
- Is generic noncancellation explicitly stated?

- Is the pole order invariant under a unit change of defining function?
- Which finite jet determines the principal coefficient?
- Can transverse derivatives enter at the claimed leading order?
- Does an even/reflection symmetry actually remove the odd term?
- Are corner claims diagonal-only or genuinely off-diagonal?

### Consolidation

- Has every unique proof supplement been transferred?
- Is any historical roadmap being cited as proof authority?
- Has the unified spine draft leaked into the proof chain?
- Is Kerr-Newman being used only as an application?
- Has Paper III remained outside the local theorem?

### Computation

- Does every verifier run from the packaged directory?
- Are scripts exact rather than floating-point confirmation?
- Are outputs byte-stable where claimed?
- Are random tests separated from symbolic proofs?
- Is there an independent replay of each headline identity?
